💡 About Me

I'm currently training as an AI Business Operations Specialist at JesTech Solution, working alongside a team of 6 specialists to build practical, hands-on skills in AI-powered operations, workflow automation, business analysis, and AI adoption strategy.

Before this, I spent time as a Healthcare Support Worker, working directly with vulnerable people in high-pressure, high-stakes environments where clear communication, patience, and rigorous process discipline weren't optional — they were the job. That background, combined with a BSc in Social Work, means I understand something a lot of AI Ops candidates don't: how change actually lands with the people who have to live with it.

What sets me apart: I bring genuine stakeholder empathy to operations work — I've seen firsthand what happens when a process is designed without the end-user in mind. I combine that people-first instinct with new AI tooling and automation skills to design workflows that organizations will actually adopt, not just tolerate.
